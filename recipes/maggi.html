Maggi

Description

Type: Instant masala noodles

Prep Time: ~2 minutes

Cooking Time: 2 minutes

Servings: 1 packet (standard)

Ingredients

  • 1 packet Maggi noodles with its Tastemaker spice sachet
  • 1.5 to 2 cups water
  • Optional additions: 1 tsp oil/butter, chopped onions, tomatoes, green chilies, or mixed vegetables (peas, carrots, capsicum).

Steps

  1. Boil Water: Pour 1.5 to 2 cups of water into a pan and bring it to a rolling boil.
  2. Add Masala: Stir in the Maggi Tastemaker spice sachet until dissolved. (Optional: sauté onions and tomatoes in a little oil first if making a vegetable version).
  3. Add Noodles: Break the noodle cake in half or keep it whole and add it to the boiling water.
  4. Cook: Simmer for about 2 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the noodles soften and absorb most of the liquid.
  5. Serve: Remove from heat and serve hot.
