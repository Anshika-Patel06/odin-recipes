Paneer Butter Masala

Description

A rich creamy curry , great in taste made with Paneer

Best serve with naan and rice

Ingredients

  • 250g Paneer
  • 3 tablespoon Butter
  • 4 tomtatoes
  • Paneer Masala
  • 4 Onions

Steps

  1. Blend the tomatoes into a puree.
  2. Melt the butter and cook the puree.
  3. Add the paneer and simmer.
