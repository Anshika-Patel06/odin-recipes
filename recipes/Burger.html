Burger

Description

A burger is a popular sandwich made of a cooked ground meat (or veggie) patty placed inside a sliced bread bun, usually topped with cheese, fresh vegetables, and sauces.

Ingredients

  • Patty: Ground beef (80/20 lean-to-fat ratio) or a veggie/potato mix
  • Bun: Hamburger or brioche bun
  • Toppings: Cheese slice (Cheddar/American), lettuce, sliced tomato, red onion, and pickles
  • Seasoning & Sauce: Salt, black pepper, ketchup, mustard, and mayonnaise

Steps

  1. Form the patty: Shape ground meat or veggie mix into a flat round patty slightly wider than the bun.
  2. Season: Sprinkle both sides generously with salt and pepper.
  3. Cook: Sear in a hot pan or grill for 3–5 minutes per side; add cheese during the last minute to melt.
  4. 1. Toast the buns: Lightly toast the inside of the burger buns in a pan.
  5. Assemble: Layer sauce, lettuce, tomato, onion, the cheesy patty, and the top bun
